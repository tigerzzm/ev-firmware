EV 2026 — Wiring Diagram (Raspberry Pi Pico 2)

Signal connections are drawn; power & ground follow the power tree in WIRING_GUIDE.md §1. Coloured pills show each block's supply. Physical Pico pin numbers in parentheses.

Before power: run the pre-power checklist in WIRING_GUIDE.md §9. The Pico 2 is not 5 V-tolerant — a 5 V encoder or a 5 V-powered LCD must go through a level shifter, never straight to a GPIO.

Full pin-by-pin tables, the power tree, decoupling, and the level-shifter details are in WIRING_GUIDE.md. This picture is the map; that doc is the territory.
